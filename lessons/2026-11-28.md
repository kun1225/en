## Vocabulary — 7 minutes

Definitions and examples are original. Comparisons describe tendencies rather than absolute rules. CEFR labels apply to the intended meanings, not automatically to derived forms.

### 1. Serene — C2 · adjective

**English:** Peaceful and calm, without visible worry or disturbance.  
**繁體中文：** 安詳的；平靜寧和的。  
**Level verification:** [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/serene) labels this meaning C2.

1. The garden felt serene in the early morning.  
   **Simpler English:** The garden felt peaceful and calm in the early morning.
2. She remained serene throughout the discussion.  
   **Simpler English:** She stayed calm and untroubled throughout the discussion.
3. Soft lighting gave the room a serene atmosphere.  
   **Simpler English:** Soft lighting made the room feel peaceful and calm.

**Word family:** serenely (adverb)：平靜地；serenity (noun)：寧靜、安詳。  
**Compare two related words:** Calm（平靜的）is broader and more everyday; tranquil（寧靜的）often describes a setting. Serene can suggest a composed, untroubled quality in a person or atmosphere; these uses overlap.

### 2. Secluded — C2 · adjective

**English:** Away from other people or places and offering privacy.  
**繁體中文：** 僻靜的；隱蔽而少人打擾的。  
**Level verification:** [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/secluded) labels this meaning C2.

1. We found a secluded bench behind the museum.  
   **Simpler English:** We found a bench away from other people behind the museum.
2. The café has a secluded courtyard.  
   **Simpler English:** The café has a courtyard hidden away from other people.
3. They chose a secluded spot for their conversation.  
   **Simpler English:** They chose a place away from other people for their conversation.

**Word family:** seclude (verb)：使隔離、使獨處；seclusion (noun)：隱居、隔離狀態。  
**Compare two related words:** Remote（偏遠的）emphasizes distance; private（私人的、不受打擾的）can concern ownership or access. A secluded courtyard can be in a busy city rather than remote.

### 3. Vibrant — C1 · adjective

**English:** Bright and strong in colour.  
**繁體中文：** 色彩鮮明的；鮮豔的。  
**Level verification:** [Oxford, Colours and Shapes topic](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/vibrant?q=vibrant) labels this meaning C1.

1. The poster uses vibrant shades of orange.  
   **Simpler English:** The poster uses strong, bright shades of orange.
2. Her vibrant blue scarf stood out against the grey coat.  
   **Simpler English:** Her strong, bright blue scarf stood out against the grey coat.
3. The artist chose vibrant colours for the entrance.  
   **Simpler English:** The artist chose strong, bright colours for the entrance.

**Word family:** vibrantly (adverb)：鮮豔地；vibrancy (noun)：鮮明、生氣。  
**Compare two related words:** Bright（明亮、鮮亮的）can concern light or colour; colourful（多彩的）can emphasize variety. Vibrant emphasizes strong, striking colour, even if only one colour dominates. It also means lively in another sense.

### 4. Exquisite — C2 · adjective

**English:** Extremely beautiful or finely made, often with delicate detail.  
**繁體中文：** 精美絕倫的；細緻優美的。  
**Level verification:** [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/exquisite) labels this meaning C2.

1. The bowl has an exquisite painted border.  
   **Simpler English:** The bowl has an exceptionally beautiful painted border.
2. She admired the exquisite detail of the carving.  
   **Simpler English:** She admired the exceptionally fine, beautiful details of the carving.
3. The exhibition includes several exquisite glass objects.  
   **Simpler English:** The exhibition includes several exceptionally beautiful glass objects.

**Word family:** exquisitely (adverb)：精美地；exquisiteness (noun)：精美（較少用）。  
**Compare two related words:** Beautiful（美麗的）is the broader everyday word; elaborate（繁複精細的）emphasizes complexity. Exquisite praises exceptional beauty or delicacy; a simple object can be exquisite without being elaborate.

### 5. Renowned — C2 · adjective

**English:** Widely known, especially for a particular quality or achievement.  
**繁體中文：** 享有盛名的；以某事聞名的。  
**Level verification:** [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/renowned) labels this meaning C2.

1. The town is renowned for its pottery.  
   **Simpler English:** The town is widely known for its pottery.
2. A renowned designer created the collection.  
   **Simpler English:** A widely known designer created the collection.
3. The school is renowned for training skilled craftspeople.  
   **Simpler English:** The school is widely known for training skilled craftspeople.

**Word family:** renown (uncountable noun)：名望、聲譽；internationally renowned（搭配）：享譽國際的。  
**Compare two related words:** Famous（有名的）does not necessarily imply respect; acclaimed（備受讚譽的）emphasizes praise. Renowned often suggests an established reputation for something; someone can be famous without being acclaimed.

## Two useful phrases — 2 minutes

### Business: Set the tone

**English:** Establish the mood or style that influences what follows.  
**繁體中文：** 定下基調；塑造氣氛。  
**Example:** The opening remarks set the tone for the client presentation.  
**Simpler English:** The opening remarks established the mood for the rest of the client presentation.  
**Pattern:** set the tone for something; can describe a positive or negative tone.

### Everyday: A change of scenery

**English:** A different setting that gives you a break from your usual surroundings.  
**繁體中文：** 換個環境；轉換心情。  
**Example:** I worked at the library because I needed a change of scenery.  
**Simpler English:** I worked at the library because I wanted a break from my usual surroundings.

## Reading — 3 minutes

### A room worth spending time in

*Original fictional article.*

After several weeks of working from her kitchen table, Nora wanted **a change of scenery**. She visited a small design museum that had recently opened a reading room. The building stood beside a busy road, but the entrance led through a narrow passage into a **secluded** courtyard. From there, the traffic sounded surprisingly distant.

Inside, a member of staff explained that a **renowned** furniture designer had helped plan the room. Nora expected something expensive and uncomfortable. Instead, she found broad wooden tables, ordinary reading lamps, and chairs that invited people to stay. The design drew attention to how the room worked rather than to the designer's name.

A large poster near the entrance used **vibrant** yellow and blue. Its strong colours helped visitors find the room without filling every wall with signs. Farther inside, the colours became softer. This gradual shift helped **set the tone** for a quieter activity.

Nora chose a seat beside a cabinet containing an **exquisite** ceramic bowl. Its painted edge was so fine that she leaned closer to see the small differences between the leaves. A short label identified the maker and explained the technique without telling visitors what they should feel.

The room felt **serene**, although it was not completely silent. Someone turned a page, a chair moved across the floor, and rain began tapping against the courtyard window. These small sounds made the space feel occupied rather than empty.

Nora stayed for an hour and finished a chapter she had struggled to read at home. On leaving, she realized that the room's appeal came from several small decisions working together. Privacy, useful light, comfortable furniture, and a few carefully chosen objects had made attention feel easier.

**理解檢查：** Why was the bright poster near the entrance useful? Why did the room feel serene without being silent?  
**繁體中文提示：** 留意入口指引與閱讀區的不同需求，以及細小聲響帶來的生活感。

## How a native speaker might describe it — 母語者會怎麼描述

**Today's random subject:** A desk lamp／一盞桌燈。  
*Imagined example: no actual lamp or image was supplied. This is one natural conversational description, not the only way a native speaker would say it.*

**Natural English:**  
It's a small desk lamp with a slightly crooked shade and a heavy metal base. It gives off a warm glow, so the corner feels cosy without being too bright. The paint's a bit chipped around the edges, but I think that gives it character.

**繁體中文：**  
那是一盞小桌燈，燈罩有點歪，底座是沉甸甸的金屬。它散發溫暖的光，讓那個角落很舒適，又不會太亮。邊緣的油漆有些剝落，不過我覺得這反而讓它別有個性。

**Simpler English:**  
It's a small desk lamp with a shade that isn't quite straight and a heavy metal base. Its warm light makes the corner feel comfortable without being too bright. Some paint has come off around the edges, but I think this makes it more interesting.

**Useful expressions:**

- **Gives off a warm glow**：散發柔暖的光。Give off 可搭配光、熱、氣味；glow 常指柔和而持續的光。
- **A bit chipped around the edges**：邊緣有些小塊剝落。Chipped 可描述油漆剝落或物品邊角缺損。
- **Gives it character**：讓它別有個性或韻味。常用來欣賞不完美的細節，不等於「品質很好」。

**Speaking prompt:** Describe a lamp or another object near you in three sentences: its shape, what it does, and one small detail you notice.
