# English lesson — November 6, 2026

Taiwan date · Making a stronger launch decision · About 12 minutes

Today’s five words help you question a proposal, explain limits, and examine evidence. Read the examples carefully, then use the comparisons to choose the word that fits your purpose. The comparisons describe common uses; similar words often overlap.

## 1. Viable — C1

**Part of speech:** adjective<br>
**Meaning:** Able to work successfully.<br>
**繁體中文：** 可行的；有成功機會的；能持續經營的。

**Verification:** [Oxford’s CEFR list](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/external/pdf/wordlists/oxford-3000-5000/The_Oxford_5000.pdf) lists viable as C1. [Cambridge’s business entry](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/viable) supports the intended meaning: able to work or succeed. The Oxford list gives a word-and-part-of-speech level, rather than a separate rating for every sense.


**Exactly two examples**

1. The subscription service will only be viable if enough customers renew after the free trial.<br>
   這項訂閱服務只有在足夠的顧客於免費試用後續訂，才有機會持續經營。
2. We need a viable alternative before we stop supporting the current product.<br>
   在停止支援現有產品之前，我們需要一個可行的替代方案。

**Two related words**

- Workable: practical enough to use — 行得通的。
- Sustainable: able to continue over time — 能持續的。

**Similar words: how to choose**

| Word | Main question | Typical phrase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Viable | Can it work successfully? 有成功機會嗎？ | a financially viable business |
| Workable | Can we put it into practice? 實際做起來行得通嗎？ | a workable compromise |
| Sustainable | Can we keep doing it? 能長久維持嗎？ | a sustainable workload |

Workable often accepts an imperfect but usable solution. Viable suits business decisions about success. Sustainable stresses continued operation or effort; in environmental contexts, it can also concern environmental harm. A business may be viable now without having a sustainable staffing model.

**Word family — derivatives**

- Viability (noun): 可行性；持續經營能力。
- Unviable (adjective): 不可行的；無法持續經營的。

**Usage:** financially viable, commercially viable, a viable alternative. The related words above are not derivatives.

## 2. Constraint — C1

**Part of speech:** countable noun<br>
**Meaning:** Something that limits what you can do.<br>
**繁體中文：** 限制；限制條件。

**Verification:** [Oxford’s CEFR list](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/external/pdf/wordlists/oxford-3000-5000/The_Oxford_5000.pdf) lists constraint as C1. [Oxford’s entry](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/constraint) supports the intended limiting-condition meaning.


**Exactly two examples**

1. Our main constraint is the number of engineers available to maintain the service.<br>
   我們主要的限制是能夠維護這項服務的工程師人數。
2. The designer simplified the checkout process to work within the project’s budget constraints.<br>
   設計師簡化了結帳流程，讓方案符合專案的預算限制。

**Two related words**

- Restriction: a rule or condition that limits an activity — 限制規定。
- Obstacle: something that makes progress difficult — 障礙。

**Similar words: how to choose**

| Word | Focus | Typical phrase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constraint | A boundary you must plan within 規劃時須考慮的限制 | budget constraints |
| Restriction | Something that limits permitted action 常指規定上的限制 | restrictions on data sharing |
| Obstacle | A difficulty blocking progress 妨礙進展的困難 | an obstacle to agreement |

Constraint is common in project planning. Restriction often suggests a rule imposed by someone, although it can describe other limits too. Obstacle presents something as a difficulty to overcome. One budget problem could be described using any of these words, with a different emphasis.

**Word family — base and derived forms**

- Constrain (verb; base): 限制；約束。
- Constrained (adjective): 受到限制的。

Constraints is a plural, not a derivative. Constrained can also be a past-tense or participle form.

**Usage:** time constraints, a constraint on growth, work within constraints.

## 3. Explicit — C1

**Part of speech:** adjective<br>
**Meaning:** Stated clearly and directly, leaving little room for doubt.<br>
**繁體中文：** 明確說明的；清楚直接的。

**Verification:** [Oxford’s CEFR list](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/external/pdf/wordlists/oxford-3000-5000/The_Oxford_5000.pdf) lists explicit as C1. [Cambridge’s explanation](https://dictionaryblog.cambridge.org/2017/07/19/simple-and-straightforward-words-meaning-clear/) supports the clear-communication meaning used here.


**Exactly two examples**

1. Please be explicit about which team owns customer support after the launch.<br>
   請明確說明產品推出後，由哪個團隊負責客戶支援。
2. The agreement requires explicit permission before either company shares customer data.<br>
   合約要求，任一公司分享客戶資料前，都必須取得明確許可。

**Two related words**

- Clear: easy to understand — 清楚的。
- Specific: giving particular details — 具體的。

**Similar words: how to choose**

| Word | Focus | Typical phrase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explicit | Directly stated, not left to guesswork 明說出來 | explicit permission |
| Clear | Easy to understand 容易理解 | a clear explanation |
| Specific | Exact details rather than general information 有具體細節 | a specific deadline |

Clear is the broadest and most natural everyday choice. Specific asks for detail. Explicit stresses that something must actually be stated. An instruction can be explicit but difficult to understand because it uses technical language; it would then need clearer wording.

**Word family — derivatives**

- Explicitly (adverb): 明確地；直接說明地。
- Explicitness (noun): 明確性；表達清楚直接的程度。Established, but less common in everyday conversation.

**Usage:** explicit instructions, explicit permission, be explicit about something.

## 4. Compelling — C1

**Part of speech:** adjective<br>
**Meaning:** So convincing that it gives people a strong reason to believe or act.<br>
**繁體中文：** 很有說服力的；令人信服的。

**Verification:** [Oxford’s CEFR list](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/external/pdf/wordlists/oxford-3000-5000/The_Oxford_5000.pdf) lists compelling as C1. [Cambridge’s STRONG meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/compelling) supports today’s persuasive use.


**Exactly two examples**

1. The customer interviews provide a compelling reason to improve the mobile experience first.<br>
   這些客戶訪談提供了很有說服力的理由，支持我們優先改善手機使用體驗。
2. Her presentation made a compelling case for keeping the simpler pricing model.<br>
   她的簡報有力地說明了保留較簡單定價模式的理由。

**Two related words**

- Persuasive: effective at making people agree — 有說服力的。
- Convincing: making something seem true or believable — 令人信服的。

**Similar words: how to choose**

| Word | Common emphasis | Typical phrase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compelling | Strong enough to demand serious attention 力道很強，值得重視 | a compelling reason |
| Persuasive | Effective at changing opinions 能說服他人 | a persuasive speaker |
| Convincing | Believable or credible 讓人相信是真的 | a convincing explanation |

All three can describe an argument. Compelling often suggests particularly strong force. Persuasive suits a person’s communication skills. Convincing often concerns whether an account seems believable. These are tendencies, not fixed boundaries; a convincing explanation may still turn out to be false.

**Word family — base and derived forms**

- Compel (verb; base): 迫使；使人不得不做某事。
- Compellingly (adverb): 令人信服地；引人入勝地。Established, but less common than compelling.

**Usage:** compelling evidence, make a compelling case for something. Compel often involves force; a compelling argument persuades through its strength.

## 5. Scrutiny — C2

**Part of speech:** uncountable noun<br>
**Meaning:** A close, careful examination of something.<br>
**繁體中文：** 仔細審查；嚴格檢視。

**Verification:** [Cambridge](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/scrutiny) explicitly marks this examination meaning C2.


**Exactly two examples**

1. The sales forecast came under scrutiny after two major customers cancelled their orders.<br>
   兩家主要客戶取消訂單後，這份銷售預測受到了仔細檢視。
2. Our cost estimates must stand up to scrutiny before we request additional funding.<br>
   在申請額外資金之前，我們的成本估算必須經得起嚴格檢驗。

**Two related words**

- Inspection: a check of condition, quality, or standards — 檢查。
- Analysis: detailed study to understand something — 分析。

**Similar words: how to choose**

| Word | Main purpose | Typical phrase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scrutiny | Examine closely, often critically 嚴格檢視 | under public scrutiny |
| Inspection | Check condition or compliance 檢查狀況或是否合規 | a safety inspection |
| Analysis | Understand parts, patterns, or causes 理解結構、模式或原因 | an analysis of sales data |

Scrutiny often suggests accountability or doubt. Inspection commonly involves a defined check. Analysis aims to explain or understand and need not involve suspicion. Scrutiny is uncountable in today’s sense; inspection and analysis can be countable or uncountable depending on use.

**Word family — derivative**

- Scrutinize / scrutinise (verb): 仔細審查；詳細檢視。American and British spelling choices.

Scrutinized and scrutinizing are verb inflections, not separate derivatives.

**Usage:** under close scrutiny, come under scrutiny, stand up to scrutiny. Avoid “a scrutiny” in this meaning.

## Business phrase 1: On the same page

**Meaning:** Sharing the same understanding of a situation or plan.<br>
**自然中文：** 有一致的理解；對事情有共識。

**When and tone:** Friendly and conversational; useful in meetings and internal emails before discussing details. Shared understanding does not always mean approval: people can understand a plan and still disagree with it.

1. Before we discuss pricing, let’s make sure we’re on the same page about what the basic plan includes.<br>
   討論價格之前，我們先確認大家對基本方案包含的內容有一致理解。
2. I’ve added a short summary so that everyone is on the same page before tomorrow’s client meeting.<br>
   我補上了一段簡短摘要，讓大家在明天的客戶會議前都有一致的理解。

**Usage:** on the same page about + topic. Name the point people need to understand.

## Business phrase 2: Leave room for

**Meaning:** Allow enough space, time, money, or flexibility for something.<br>
**自然中文：** 為……預留空間或餘裕；保留……的可能性。

**When and tone:** Neutral and cooperative; useful during planning or negotiation. Explain what needs the extra capacity so that your request has a clear purpose.

1. The delivery schedule needs to leave room for a second round of customer testing.<br>
   交付時程需要預留第二輪客戶測試的時間。
2. We can accept the lower price if the contract leaves room for an annual review.<br>
   如果合約保留每年檢討的空間，我們可以接受較低的價格。

**Usage:** leave room for + noun or -ing form.

**Phrase distinction:** On the same page concerns shared understanding; leave room for concerns flexibility or capacity. A team can understand a schedule perfectly while still needing more time in it.

## Reading practice: A smaller launch, a better decision

An original fictional business passage.

Maya’s team had six weeks to launch a new scheduling product. The first proposal included shared calendars, automated reminders, and detailed reports. Sales liked the feature list, but engineering had only three people available. Their main **constraint** was maintenance capacity, not the ability to build a demonstration.

At Monday’s meeting, Maya asked everyone to get **on the same page** about the goal. The launch needed to show that small businesses would pay for easier scheduling. It did not need to prove that the team could build every feature customers mentioned.

The research lead presented a **compelling** case for a smaller release. Most interview participants struggled with missed appointments. Few needed detailed reports. However, the finance manager questioned whether the service would be **viable** at the proposed price. If each customer needed hours of personal support, subscription income might not cover the cost.

Instead of defending the original forecast, Maya invited closer **scrutiny**. The team checked its assumptions about support requests, renewal rates, and payment fees. The revised figures suggested that a limited release could work, provided that setup instructions were easy to follow.

Maya then made the responsibilities **explicit**. Engineering would own reliability, customer support would track setup problems, and research would interview customers who stopped using the service. The schedule would **leave room for** changes after the first week.

The team approved a release with calendars and reminders. They postponed reports until customers showed a clear need for them. The decision gave them a focused question to answer: would the product save enough time for customers to keep paying?

**繁體中文摘要：** 團隊受到維護人力限制，因此縮小產品推出範圍。他們先確認共同目標，再檢視訪談與財務假設，明確分配責任，並預留調整時間。這次推出的重點，是驗證客戶是否願意持續付費。

**Check your understanding:** Why was maintenance capacity more important than building a demonstration? Which assumption could make the service financially unsuccessful?

## Worth learning

- Make a compelling case for + noun / -ing：有力地說明支持某事的理由。
- Be explicit about + topic：明確說明某個主題。
- Stand up to scrutiny：經得起仔細檢驗。
- Leave room for + noun / -ing：為某事預留餘裕。
